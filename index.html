About

Join us for the second edition of the Urban and Spatial PhD Workshop. This event aims to gather young minds (PhD students or postdocs) in urban economics and related fields. The workshop is organized around 14 presentations, followed by an informal discussion by a fellow presenter and audience Q&A. We are delighted to have Gabriel Loumeau (University of Neuchâtel, CESifo, and CEPR) as our keynote speaker.

Participation is free of charge but registration is mandatory. If you are interested to join, please fill this survey before December 1st, 2026. We acknowledge the generous funding and support from EUR Data EFM, CREST and ENSAE.
