About

Join us for the second edition of the Urban and Spatial PhD Workshop. This event aims to gather young minds (PhD students) in urban economics and related fields. The workshop is organized around 8 presentations of 30' each, followed by an informal discussion by a fellow presenter and audience Q&A. A keynote lecture will be given by Gabriel Loumeau (University of Neuchâtel, CESifo, and CEPR).

Apply before October 1st 2026 (call for paper) and spread the word!

We acnowledge the generous funding and support from CREST, ENSAE, and ANR EUR grants.
